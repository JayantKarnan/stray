HOW TO PLAY

CONTROLS

WASD / Arrows — move & turn

Shift / RUN — toggle sprint

Space / JUMP — jump (hold for jetpack)

B / BARK — bark, scare the locals

E / RAM — headbutt & charge

G / GRAB — grab a prop or mob, tap again to fling

R / FLOP — go ragdoll

M / MUT — cycle mutators (giant, moon-gravity…)

Esc — pause
